Case Study #2

COSRX

Redesigning an e-commerce experience to improve usability, minimalism, transparency, and trust.

Role: UX/UI Designer

Methods: Heuristic evaluation, competitive analysis

Tools: Figma, VS Code, Chrome DevTools

Timeline: 2 weeks

01

The Challenge

Does COSRX's digital experience reflect the same simplicity, transparency, and trust as its brand?

COSRX is built around a minimalist approach to skincare. I wanted to explore whether those same principles carried into its digital experience and identify opportunities to improve usability without compromising the brand's visual identity.

02

The Problem

A heuristic evaluation of two key pages uncovered three recurring usability problems.

Difficult
to Scan

Important content competed with secondary information.

Unclear
Interactions

Weak Content
Hierarchy

The interface didn't always prioritize the information users needed to understand products or make decisions.

03

Why It Mattered

Clear, transparent information helps users make more informed decisions.

The goal wasn't simply to make COSRX's pages look cleaner. It was to create an experience that better aligned the interface with the brand's values while helping users find information, understand products, and make decisions with confidence.

Research

Evaluating the experience through Nielsen's usability heuristics

I evaluated two key COSRX pages against Nielsen's 10 usability heuristics, documenting issues based on their potential impact on users and prioritizing the highest-severity findings for redesign.

Design Principles

01

Clarity

Make important information easier to find and understand.

02

Consistency

Create predictable visual patterns for interactive elements.

03

Hierarchy

Prioritize content based on what users need to see and accomplish first.

04

Trust

Surface critical information that helps users make confident decisions.

Design Decisions

01

Bringing Critical Information Into View

Important information was difficult to locate.

I moved critical information, including COSRX's scam warning, from the bottom of the page to a more prominent position near the top. I also strengthened its visual hierarchy so users could recognize the message without having to search for it.

02

Making Interactions More Predictable

Interactive elements did not consistently communicate how they behaved.

I standardized the visual treatment of interactive elements and removed misleading interaction cues so links and controls more clearly communicated when they could be used.

Clarity · Consistency

Before

After

03

Simplifying the Information Hierarchy

Important content competed with secondary information.

I reorganized the page structure to create a clearer visual path and give greater emphasis to the information users need when evaluating products. Secondary content was reduced, regrouped, or repositioned so key product details and actions could be identified more easily.

Clarity · Hierarchy

Before

After

04

Improving the Quick Buy Experience

The Quick Buy interface became obscured by overlapping elements.

I redesigned the Quick Buy interaction so essential product information and controls remained visible when the feature was activated. The goal was to make the interaction clearer and more predictable instead of introducing additional visual friction.

Expected Impact

A clearer experience built around transparency, consistency, and trust.

The redesign was not tested after implementation, so these outcomes remain design hypotheses. However, each design decision directly addresses usability issues identified during the heuristic evaluation.

Easier to find

Critical product and safety information.

Easier to use

Clearer and more predictable interactions.

Easier to trust

Greater visibility and transparency throughout the experience.
