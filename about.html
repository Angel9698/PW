About

I found UX by realizing what kind of problems I wanted to solve.

UX design wasn't the career path I originally expected to follow. I began college pursuing chemical engineering, but over time I realized I was more interested in solving problems around how people understand and interact with products, systems, and everyday experiences.

That shift eventually led me to UX design—a field where research, problem-solving, and visual communication come together in a way that feels both analytical and creative.

Why UX

Clarity is what keeps me interested in design.

I value experiences that feel intentional, understandable, and considerate of the person using them. What I enjoy most is identifying where an experience creates unnecessary friction and finding a way to make that interaction feel simpler without stripping away what makes it meaningful.

That mindset is what I try to bring into my work: understand the problem first, make deliberate decisions, and let each design choice serve a clear purpose.

Personal Identity

A personal identity shaped by intention.

I created the Angel wordmark from my first name as a small personal design project. The colors shift across the mark to represent a transition from negative to positive, while the facial details come together to form a subtle happy expression. For me, it reflects what I value in design: clarity, positivity, and purpose.
